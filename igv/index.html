Pangenome4 — variants vs. ref (IGV.js)

Reference = simulated 2-chromosome genome (chr1, chr2; 10 kb each). Each variant (div01..div15) was aligned to ref with minimap2 -ax asm20; variants called with paftools.js call.
Tracks per variant:
BAM = alignment of variant chromosomes to ref
VCF = SNPs + indels called from the alignment
